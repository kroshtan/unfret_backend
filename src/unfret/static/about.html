About Unfret

Unfret checks the seller before you pay. When you ask, it reads a web shop, a marketplace listing or an ad and tells you in plain words what it noticed, and where each finding comes from.

What it does

  • On web shops and marketplaces, including third-party sellers on the big marketplaces: how old the shop's domain is, whether it names a company the EU's VAT register knows, whether a "was" price was ever really charged, countdowns and pressure tricks, reviews that also appear on other shops, and product photos that are used by other shops too.
  • On second-hand ads and tradespeople's websites: signs of an ad posted many times, payment pushed off the platform, or one call centre behind many "local" businesses.
  • For AI agents: the same check as one call, paid per check over x402 or with a prepaid pass, and an MCP server. See Developers and AI agents.

You can use it as a browser extension for Chrome and Firefox, or paste or share a link into the link checker on any phone.

Signals, not verdicts

Unfret never calls a shop a scam. A result describes what one page showed when it was checked: risk signals, each with its source, and what they usually mean. It can be wrong, in both directions, and the choice to buy is yours. If you run a shop and think a result about it is wrong, tell us.

Who makes it

Unfret is made by Floris van Beers, a one-person company in the Netherlands (Kroshtan). One person builds and runs it.

How it treats you

  • It reads a page only when you click Check. No account: an Unfret pass holds your checks.
  • The pages you check aren't stored or linked to you. Details are in the privacy statement.
  • The language model that reads pages runs in the EU, at Scaleway. Our own server is in the EU too.
  • No ads, no trackers and no affiliate links. This website loads nothing from anyone else.

Open source

The code is open source under the MIT licence: the service, website and MCP server, the browser extension and the Android app. Anyone can read how a check works, or run their own. The scoring model in the code is an open baseline; unfret.net runs privately retrained versions of it. Not published: the labelled pages the model learns from (they include pages and reviews we may not redistribute) and the collection of product-photo fingerprints.

How Unfret makes money

From the checks people and agents buy, and nothing else: no ads, no affiliate links to shops, no selling data, and no money from shops. How Unfret makes money explains it.
