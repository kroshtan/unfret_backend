Affiliates

Recommend Unfret to the people who follow you, and earn {{commission_pct}}% of what they pay for their checks, for {{months}} months. They get {{bonus_pct}}% more checks on their first pack.

Unfret never earns from shops

Unfret checks web shops, ads and companies for the people about to pay them. It has no affiliate links to shops and no paid placements, and no shop can pay to change a result. The affiliate program pays people who recommend Unfret, out of what Unfret's own users pay for their checks. Nothing else.

How it works

  1. You get a code and a link, like unfret.net/?ref=YOURCODE. The link works on any page of this site.
  2. Someone follows your link. This site keeps your code in their browser (no cookie, nothing sent to anyone) until they get an Unfret pass: when they install the extension, check a link, or buy checks. Or they type your code at checkout. That pass now counts for you.
  3. They get {{bonus_pct}}% more checks on the first pack they buy, at the same price.
  4. You earn {{commission_pct}}% of the net revenue of every pack bought on that pass for {{months}} months: what they paid, after VAT and the payment provider's fees. Card and crypto alike.

The fine print, so there are no surprises:

  • A pass counts for the first affiliate it came through, and only if it had never bought checks before. Nobody can claim someone who already was a customer, and a pass is never moved to another affiliate.
  • Buying through your own link is fine, at the same {{commission_pct}}%.
  • A refunded purchase earns nothing: a refund or a chargeback takes back the commission on it.

Payouts

  • Monthly, by hand: by bank transfer, PayPal or crypto, as you prefer.
  • After {{hold_days}} days. Commission counts as payable {{hold_days}} days after the purchase (the refund window), so most refunds are settled before you're paid.
  • From {{min_payout}}. A payout is made once you have at least that much to come in a currency (dollars and euros are kept apart, never converted). Less than that carries over to the next month.
  • A refund after a payout (a late chargeback, say) leaves a negative balance, which comes off your next payouts. Nothing is lost either way.
  • When we stop working together, everything you earned is paid out, whatever the amount; from then on no new passes count for you, and the people you brought keep their bonus.

Your numbers, without an account

You get a private link to your own page: per month, how many passes came through you, how many of them made a first check, how many packs were bought, and your commission earned, taken back after refunds, and paid; and what your next payout will be, what is still in the {{hold_days}}-day hold, and what carries over. Never who: you don't see passes, buyers or anything anyone checked, and neither does anyone else. Keep the link to yourself; if it leaks, we replace it.

Say that you earn from it

The law requires it, and your audience deserves it. Wherever you share your link or code:

  • Say it plainly, up front: "I earn a commission if you buy Unfret checks through my link", or "#ad", "#affiliate", "#advertentie" at the start of the post or video, not hidden in a list of tags or below "more". In a video, say it too, not only in the description.
  • In the United States, the FTC's Endorsement Guides apply: the disclosure must be clear and conspicuous, close to the recommendation, in the same language.
  • In the Netherlands, the Reclame Code (and its Social Media & Influencer Marketing code) and the ACM's rules apply: make the advertising nature recognisable at once, e.g. "#advertentie" or "#ad" at the start.
  • Be accurate. Unfret shows signals and where they come from; it can be wrong, and it never says a shop is a scam. Don't promise more, don't name a real shop as a scam on Unfret's behalf, and don't post fake reviews or results.
  • No spam, no ads that bid on "Unfret", no pretending to be Unfret.

We may switch off an affiliate who breaks these rules; commission already earned honestly is still paid.
