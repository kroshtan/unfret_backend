Unfret for Android

Share a link to Unfret from any app and check it on your phone itself. The page opens inside Unfret, over your own connection, and is read on your phone when you tap Check this page.

What it does

  • Share a link from any app: your browser, Marktplaats, Vinted, eBay, Facebook or a shop's own app. Tap Share, then Check with Unfret. Or paste a link in the app.
  • The page opens on your phone, over your own connection, so shops that turn servers away still show it, and you can deal with a cookie banner or a captcha yourself.
  • It reads the page on your phone when you tap Check this page, with the same code as the browser extension, and tells you in plain words whether it all adds up.
  • Checks are free: no account, no pass, nothing sold in the app, like the extension and the link checker.

Install the APK

Only for the APK: from Google Play, Play installs it for you.

  1. Download the APK on your phone. Your browser may warn that this kind of file can harm your device: that's said of every app from outside Google Play. Tap Download anyway.
  2. Open the file from the download notification, or from Files → Downloads.
  3. Allow this one source. Android asks whether your browser (or Files) may install apps: tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, go back, and tap Install. If Play Protect says it doesn't know the app, choose to install it anyway or to scan it first.
  4. Turn it off again. In Settings → Apps → Special app access → Install unknown apps, pick the browser (or Files) and turn Allow from this source off. Unfret stays installed. The names differ a little between phones; searching Settings for "unknown apps" finds it.

It needs {{min_android}} or newer.

Updates

Installed from Google Play, Play updates it. The APK doesn't update itself: when a new beta is out, download it from this page and open it like the first time, and it installs over the old one. That works because every version, on Play and here, is signed with the same key, and each new one has a higher version number: an APK installs over the Play version, and Play takes over again once its version is newer.

If Android ever refuses an update because it conflicts with the installed app, that file was not signed by Unfret: don't uninstall Unfret to make it fit. (A test build you made yourself from the source code is signed with another key: uninstall that one first.)

Privacy

  • Nothing is read until you tap Check this page. Loading a page sends nothing to Unfret.
  • Sent, only to unfret.net: the page address, its product facts and visible text (account menus, forms, checkout and addresses that aren't the business's own are removed on your phone first). No pass, no account, no device details, no location, no contacts.
  • Kept on your phone: that you accepted the intro. The pages you open are forgotten when you close them.
  • No ads, no trackers, no analytics or crash reporting, no Google Play Services.

The app's full statement is its privacy policy; what the server keeps is on Privacy.
