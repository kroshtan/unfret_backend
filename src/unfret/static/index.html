The tricks Unfret catches

The same few tricks turn up on shop after shop. Each one is easy to miss when you're about to pay, and each one is something Unfret checks for you.

  • Sale ends in 09:59

    A countdown that starts again

    “Offer ends in 10 minutes”, and when you come back tomorrow the clock is back at ten minutes.

    Unfret notices timers that restart and the tools shops use to add them.

  • We're closing down: everything must go!

    The closing-down story

    A shop that's always in its last days, retiring or clearing stock, so you buy before it's “gone”.

    Unfret spots never-ending closing-down sales and other pressure in the page's words.

  • $89.99 $19.99 −78%

    A “was” price nobody paid

    A big discount measured against a price the shop never charged, or a “recommended” price.

    Unfret compares the “was” price with the prices it has seen for that product, and flags discounts too deep to be true.

  • Free shipping · delivery 15–30 business days

    Shipped from far away, in the small print

    The shop looks local, but the goods come from overseas: weeks of waiting, and returns sent abroad.

    Unfret reads the shipping and returns pages for delivery times and where returns go.

  • ★★★★★ “Great product!” ×3

    Reviews that were never written here

    The same review word for word, reviews imported from other sites, or more ratings than a new product could have.

    Unfret looks for repeated and imported reviews, and reviews it has seen on other shops.

  • Brand: XQZEUL

    Factory goods under a made-up brand

    A generic product with a brand nobody has heard of, often with the same photo as on other shops, at several times the price.

    Unfret checks whether the brand exists and whether the photo appears on other shops.

  • A family business since 1998

    “Since 1998”, online since last month

    A long history on the page, on a web address that was registered a few weeks ago.

    Unfret looks up when the address was registered and compares it with the story.

  • Shipping protection +$2.99

    Extras ticked for you

    A paid extra already ticked at checkout, or a total that's higher than the price on the page.

    Unfret flags pre-ticked extras and a checkout that costs more than the page said.

The examples above are made up; the tricks are real, seen again and again on real shops. Unfret describes what a page shows and lets you decide: it never calls a shop names.
