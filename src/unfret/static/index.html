One click, a calm answer

Unfret works on Amazon, bol.com and most web shops, including the ones you've never heard of.

  1. Find something you like

    Open its product page, wherever you shop.

  2. Click Unfret

    It reads the page only when you click, and only that page.

  3. Read it in plain words

    “Looks fine”, or a few things worth a second look. Never a cryptic score.

The homework, done for you

Who's behind the shop

The real product

Honest prices

Time to decide

No countdowns that reset, no extras ticked for you, no costs that appear at the very end.

Private by design

Unfret is there to put your mind at ease, not to watch you shop.

    For developers and AI agents

    Shopping agents can ask Unfret before they pay: one HTTP call per check over x402, in USDC or SOL, with no account. The answer is an action (allow, warn or block) and the evidence behind it. Open source, MIT licensed.
