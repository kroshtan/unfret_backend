How Unfret makes money

Shoppers' checks are free and run on donations; developers and AI agents pay for theirs. Nothing else.

What pays for Unfret

  • Donations from people who want free checks to keep running: a one-off payment, with nothing in return (no checks, no perks). See Support Unfret. If a month's donations run out, free deep checks pause until the next month; quick checks keep working.
  • Checks for developers: a developer pass comes with free checks for trying Unfret out, then packs of checks: 25 checks for $6.50 (€6.50 in the euro area), or 100 checks for $21 (€21), by card or iDEAL | Wero (sold through Polar), or with a Solana wallet. Card prices include tax; the crypto price ($5 or $20) includes VAT and is never more than by card. No subscription, and checks never expire.
  • Pay per check for AI agents: an agent pays for each check as it makes it, over x402, or uses a prepaid pass. Prices are on Developers and AI agents.

What doesn't

  • No ads, on this website, in the extension or in a result.
  • No affiliate links to shops; Unfret never earns from shops. It earns nothing when you buy from a shop, and loses nothing when you don't.
  • No selling data. The pages you check aren't kept or linked to you, so there is nothing to sell, and we wouldn't (see Privacy).
  • No money from shops. A shop can't pay for a better result, to be left out, or to learn who checked it. A shop owner who thinks a result is wrong can tell us, for free, and is judged on the facts like anyone else.

Where the money goes

To what a check costs to run: the language model that reads the page, the server, and the fees for payments. The rest keeps Unfret going.
