For shop owners and sellers

Think an Unfret result about your shop, listing or ad is wrong? Want it corrected, or want Unfret to remove what it keeps about your site? Tell us below, or write to hello@unfret.net.

What a result is

Unfret never calls a shop a scam. A result describes what one page showed when someone checked it: risk signals, each with its source (for instance the domain's registration date from the registry), and what they usually mean. It is shown only to the person who ran the check, and is not stored. Two exceptions:

  • A result someone chose to share as a link stays up for 90 days. Its page has a Take it down for review button: it is hidden at once, and we look at it.
  • A page someone reported as wrong is reviewed and may be kept, without anything about who reported it, as an example to improve the checks.

What Unfret keeps about shops

Only what any visitor of your site can see, and never who checked it or which page:

  • Site fingerprints: the first and last day a check saw your site, its platform and apps, the shape of its template, and the account IDs on its pages (analytics, ad pixels, payment accounts), to recognise shops run by one operator. Kept 400 days after the last check.
  • Contact details on your pages (e-mail addresses, phone numbers, postal address, e-mail domain): only as a keyed one-way hash, never shown to anyone. Kept 180 days after the last check.
  • Registry facts: the domain's registration date, its first certificate and the VAT check. Kept a week.
  • Prices of products with a public product number or barcode, under one-way hashes, to tell whether a "was" price was ever charged. Kept 90 days.
  • Review and photo fingerprints: one-way hashes of longer reviews (90 days) and fingerprints of the main product photo with its address on your site (400 days), to spot the same reviews or photos on other shops.
  • For a private seller's ad: a fingerprint of the ad's text and the phone numbers or e-mail addresses in it as a keyed hash, never your name. Kept 90 days.

The privacy statement has the details.

What we do with your request

  • A wrong result: we check the result against your page. If Unfret got it wrong, we correct the check itself, so every shopper gets the corrected result, and we tell you what we changed.
  • Removal: we delete your site's fingerprints and the contact details you name, wherever they are stored. The other records above (registry facts, prices, reviews, photos) expire on their own. If someone checks your site again later, Unfret sees it again, as any visitor would.
  • An objection to how we use data about your site: we stop unless we have a good reason to keep it that outweighs yours, and tell you which.

We answer within a month at the latest. A shared result you take down is hidden at once.
