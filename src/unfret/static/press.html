Fact sheet

What it is
Unfret checks the seller before you pay. It reads a web shop, a marketplace listing, a second-hand ad or a tradesperson's website when you ask, and shows the risk signals it finds, each with its source. It never calls a shop a scam.
For whom
Online shoppers, and AI agents that buy or recommend things: they pay per check over x402 (USDC or SOL on Solana) or use an MCP server.
Where
A browser extension for Chrome and Firefox, a link checker that works on any phone, and an API. In English.
Price
Packs of checks, no subscription; checks never expire. See Buy checks. Agents pay per check. How Unfret makes money.
Privacy
No account. A page is read only when you ask, and isn't stored. No ads, trackers or affiliate links to shops. The language model runs in the EU, at Scaleway.
Open source
MIT licence: source code.
Made by
Floris van Beers, a one-person company in the Netherlands (Kroshtan). About Unfret.
