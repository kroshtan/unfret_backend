Support Unfret

Unfret's checks are free for shoppers, quick and deep, with no account. A donation keeps them that way.

What a donation pays for

A deep check has a language model read the page, and that costs money each time. Free deep checks run on donations: when a month's run out, they pause until the 1st of the next month, while quick checks keep working.

What you get

Nothing in return, on purpose: no checks, no perks, no account. A donation is a one-off payment, any amount you like. It isn't linked to anything you check.
