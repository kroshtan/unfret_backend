2. No account: your pass

Unfret has no accounts. Shoppers' checks need no pass at all. Checks that are bought (for the API, the MCP server or apps) are held by an Unfret pass (developers know it as an API key) or by a Solana wallet you sign in with. Whoever has your pass can use its checks, so keep it to yourself. Keep the pass's backup code too: we store neither in readable form and can't recover a pass without it.

3. Checks

  • One check reads one page and gives you a result. Quick checks and deep checks are free for shoppers. Free deep checks run on donations: we may pause them, for instance until the next day once a day's share is used up, and we may limit how many come from one place.
  • A bought check is used per page. A page Unfret can't read uses no check, and checking the same page again with the same pass within the hour is usually free. Bought checks come in packs. There is no subscription, and checks don't expire.
  • A donation is a gift: it buys nothing (no checks, no perks) and isn't refunded.
  • Free checks have no cash value and can't be exchanged or refunded.
  • We may change prices and packs for future purchases. Checks you already have keep working.

4. Paying

  • By card, iDEAL | Wero or another method through Polar. Polar sells you the pack as merchant of record: you buy from Polar, under Polar's terms, and Polar's refund rules apply. If Polar refunds a purchase (in full or in part), the same share of its checks is taken off your pass.
  • With SOL or USDC (Solana): a payment on a blockchain can't be reversed. Crypto purchases are final and not refundable once the checks are on your pass.
  • AI agents paying per check (x402): each check is paid when it is made. If a paid check fails on our side, its price is added as checks to the paying wallet's balance instead of being sent back on the blockchain.

5. Signals, not verdicts

Unfret never calls a shop a scam. A result is an automatic reading of what one page showed when it was checked: risk signals with their sources, and what they usually mean. It is not a statement of fact about a shop, a seller or a person, and not legal, financial or purchasing advice. It can be wrong: it can miss a risk, or warn about a shop that is fine, and pages change. The decision to buy, and what you do with a result, is yours.

If you share a result, you share it as what it is: Unfret's automatic reading on a given day, not a judgement of the shop. Shop owners who think a result is wrong can tell us.
