About this lamp

A small lamp for a nightstand or a shelf: a turned oak base with a frosted glass globe that gives a soft, even light. We make the bases in our workshop from oak bought from a mill in Oregon; the globes come from a glassworks in Ohio.

  • Height 9 in, globe 5 in across, weight 1.6 lb
  • E12 bulb included: 4 W LED, 2700 K warm white, 300 lumens
  • 6 ft fabric cord with an in-line switch, US plug, UL-listed parts
  • The oak is finished with a natural oil; each base's grain is a little different

Shipping and returns

We ship within the US with USPS or UPS, with tracking. Orders placed by 1 pm Pacific ship the same or the next business day, and arrive in 2-5 business days. Shipping is $6, and free on orders over $75.
