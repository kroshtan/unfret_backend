Nothing to buy and no account. Click Unfret on a product, a second-hand ad, or the website of a tradesperson or local service, and it gives you a risk level from 0 to 4, with the reasons, in plain words.

Your first check

  1. Pin Unfret. Click the puzzle piece in your browser's toolbar and pin Unfret, so it's one click away.
  2. Open a page you're thinking of buying from. Not sure where to start? Try the Unfret test shop: every trick on it is copied from real listings.
  3. Click the Unfret icon and choose “Check this page”. Unfret reads that one page, only when you click. What is sent, and what isn't.

Free deep checks run on donations. If you'd like to help keep them free: Support Unfret.

Developers: using a pass with bought checks

A developer pass's bought checks work in the extension too. Copy the pass from your developer pass on the device where you have it, click the Unfret icon and paste it in the settings. Lost it? Its backup code gets you a new one there.
