Signing in with a key you have

  1. Pin Unfret. Click the puzzle piece in your browser's toolbar and pin Unfret, so it's one click away.
  2. Click the Unfret icon, then “Sign in with an API key or wallet” and choose API key.
  3. Paste your key and click “Use this key”. With a Solana wallet, choose a wallet instead, or sign in here.

Lost your key? Your recovery code (ufr_…) gets you a new one with the same checks under Top up my key.

Then, on a product page

Click the Unfret icon and choose “Check this page”. Unfret reads that one page, only when you click, and answers in plain words. One check per page. What is sent, and what isn't.
